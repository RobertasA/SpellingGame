# Changelog

## Little Star Witch 2.2

**Keys first, phonics second**
- The levels are in a new order. The first ten are about finding the keys: each brings in two to
  four new letters (`s a t p`, then `i n`, `m d`, `g o`, `c k`, `e u`, `r h`, `b f l`, `j v w`,
  `x y z q`) and uses only words spelt one letter per sound with letters already met. The banner
  at the start of a level names the new keys.
- Phonics starts at level 11 with the gentlest "two letters, one sound" spellings
  (`ck ff ll ss zz`), then `sh ch`, `th ng` and onwards. Nothing was dropped: the 24 phonics and
  spelling groups now share 20 levels, so a few later levels cover more.
- The whole of the Cottage Garden shows one star at a time, and the keyboard levels give longer
  to type each word.
- Brewing in the keyboard levels uses words of four letters or fewer, and the star challenge
  (hear it, then spell it) only asks for a two- or three-letter word.
- Saved stars stay with their level number, so after this update a star may sit on a level whose
  words have changed. "Start again from the beginning" on the grown-ups page clears them.

**Keyboard**
- The on-screen keyboard stays out of the way while a real keyboard is being typed on, in the
  star challenge as well as when flying. Touching the screen while spelling brings it back.
  When the challenge keyboard is hidden, a stuck child sees the next letter faintly in its place.

## Little Star Witch 2.1

**Things to find**
- Eighteen woodland visitors (three per place, one of them shy) pop out of hiding places in the
  middle of a level and hop along. The map shows who has been found and who is still hiding;
  a couple of old friends tag along at the start of every level.
- Every level of a place is flown at a different time of day, with its scenery laid out afresh.

**Reading and spelling**
- Words to type no longer have a picture beside them, so they have to be read. The picture pops
  out as the reward once the word is spelled.
- Brewing ends with a star challenge: the word is heard and pictured but never shown, and is
  written letter by letter on a whole keyboard. Grown-ups can choose "always look first" or
  "challenges only".

**Voice**
- The voice-over can be turned off (🗣️ on the title screen, the pause card and while playing) so
  the child does the reading.
- Your own recordings replace the computer voice. The Voice studio page records every word and
  sentence in the browser, trims the silence, and exports or imports a zip.

**Looks**
- New renderer: soft shadows, glow, filmic colour, textured fur / wood / stone / cloth, swaying
  leaves and grass, rippling water, instanced ground cover, drifting fireflies, snow and leaves.
- Three graphics levels, stepping down by itself on slower devices.

**Not yet tried on real hardware**: frame rate on tablets, real speech voices, and recording with a
real microphone in Safari and Firefox were not available where this was tested.
