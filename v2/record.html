A few friendly tips

  • Find a quiet room, and hold the phone or microphone a hand's width from your mouth.
  • Say each word clearly and a little slowly, as you would to your child. For a word, say just the word.
  • Sentences are best read warmly, like a bedtime story.
  • You can stop any time. Every recording is saved the moment you finish it, so you can carry on another day.

Keep your recordings safe

Recordings live in this browser. To use them on other devices, download them as a zip, then load that zip on the other device, or unzip it into the game's v2/voice/ folder.
