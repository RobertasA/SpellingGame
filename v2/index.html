For grown-ups

Little Star Witch practises spelling in two ways. Flying: read a word and type it to send a lost star home (letter recognition, keyboard skills, reading with sound buttons). Brewing: look at a word, cover it, then build it from its sounds (the "look, cover, write, check" routine used in schools). A ⭐ star challenge word is never shown: your child hears it, sees its picture, and writes it on a keyboard. The first ten levels are about finding the keys: a few new letters at a time, in short words where every letter is its own sound. From level 11 the levels loosely follow the order of phonics teaching in Reception and the Year 1 and Year 2 spelling content of the National Curriculum for England. Nothing can be lost or failed — a star that isn't spelled in time simply hides and comes back. Words to type are shown without a picture, so they have to be read; the picture pops out as the reward. Little woodland visitors pop out now and then and come along — three live in each place.
